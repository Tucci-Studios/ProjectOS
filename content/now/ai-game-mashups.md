In the last month a wave of videos has shown one game running inside another. Minecraft's world rendered in Elden Ring's engine drew about 23 million views. Skate 3's board physics inside Modern Warfare. Mario fighting Elden Ring bosses. Mario 64 with Portal's portals.

How it's done matters more than the clips. The builders are using Claude to help *decompile* games: turn the shipped program back into readable code, then change it. A technique called a pass-through mod runs two games at once and has one mirror the other. In a few cases the whole game was recompiled to run in a web browser.

Two honest cautions. First, several of the most viral clips are unverified prototypes, and the best-known creator says years of prior human reverse-engineering work came before the AI. Second, the legal position is bad. Taking a game apart to understand it has some protection. Distributing a rebuilt copy of someone's game does not, and Nintendo removed hundreds of related repositories in August. The GTA V browser port was taken down within hours.

So: watch, learn how it works, and understand why it's exciting. Don't expect the downloads to stay up, and don't be the one hosting them.
