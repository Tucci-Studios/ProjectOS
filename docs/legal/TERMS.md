# Project OS — Terms of Service (v0 DRAFT)

> **STATUS: TEMPLATE DRAFT — NOT LAWYER-REVIEWED.**
> Written 2026-08-02. Get professional legal review before or shortly after
> public launch. This draft is deliberately plain-English.

## 1. What Project OS is

Project OS is a directory and community for open-source projects. **We do not
host code or binaries.** All code lives on GitHub under GitHub's own Terms of
Service. Project OS stores project metadata, fork lineage, and community
content (posts, comments, likes).

## 2. The Open Agreement (accepted before your first publish)

By publishing a project on Project OS you agree:

1. **It's open source.** Your repo carries a valid open-source license
   (GPL-3.0 recommended) and the license in your `project.json` matches it.
2. **The no-sell rule.** Projects published on Project OS may not be sold,
   paywalled, or gated behind payment — here or as the published artifact.
   Forks in a Project OS lineage stay free and open. (What you do with
   separate commercial work outside the platform is your business.)
3. **No malware, ever.** No malicious, deceptive, or privacy-violating code.
   Obfuscated payloads, miners, stealers, spyware — instant delisting and ban.
4. **You have the rights.** You own or are licensed to publish everything in
   the repo, and forks respect the parent's license.
5. **Lineage is public and permanent.** Fork relationships, base names, and
   attribution are displayed publicly and survive even if you delist.

## 3. Our rights

- We can **delist any project or remove any content at any time**, including
  on valid DMCA or license-violation complaints. Delisting removes it from
  Project OS; your GitHub repo is untouched (that's between you and GitHub).
- We can suspend or ban accounts that violate these terms.
- Community moderation roles (trusted reviewers) act under these same rules.

## 4. Your risk

- **Read before you run.** Projects are community-submitted code. Scan badges
  (VirusTotal, CodeQL) are signals, not guarantees. You run what you download
  at your own risk.
- The platform is provided as-is, no warranties, liability limited to the
  maximum extent permitted by law. *(Lawyer: proper limitation-of-liability +
  indemnification language needed here.)*

## 5. Content rules (Uplink, comments, tutorials)

No harassment, spam, scams, or illegal content. All posted HTML/markdown is
sanitized; attempts to smuggle scripts into posts are treated as attacks.
Report anything with the report button; a human reviews every report.

## 6. Changes

We'll announce material changes on Uplink 14 days before they take effect.
